About

I've loved footy for as long as I can remember. Working in the game was always the dream, but growing up in rural Western Australia, it never really felt possible. Now I'm going after it, and this site is a big part of that.

What this site is

Don't Argue Media is my personal portfolio. I want to work in AFL analytics, and the best way I know to show what I can do is to actually do it. Everything here is something I've built, tested and rebuilt, usually more than once.

None of it is finished. I'm learning as I go, and the models get better every time someone tells me what's wrong with them.

How it started

It started with one model: Wins Above Replacement. Baseball has had a single number for what a player is worth for years, and I wanted to see if footy could have one too. WAR puts a number on how many extra wins a player adds compared with a fringe player at his position.

From there it kept growing. There are now five more models: VFL WAR, the Stat Explorer, the AFL Readiness Score, and player radars for both the AFL and VFL. Alongside them are the deep dives, where I take one question and dig into it properly.

Why it's free

Footy stats can feel like a bit of a closed shop. The good stuff tends to sit with clubs, broadcasters and paid services, and what's left for everyone else is often hard to read and even harder to use.

I want this to be the opposite: free, easy to use and actually fun. Whether you're a fan settling a debate with your mates, someone working at a club, or anyone else involved in footy, you should be able to jump in, have a look around and get something out of it.

Whether you work in footy, data or you're just keen to chat about the numbers, feel free to reach out. And if you reckon the model's got someone wrong, tell me. That's half the fun.
