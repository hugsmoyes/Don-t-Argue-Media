AFL Readiness Score

Every VFL player · Chance of playing AFL next season · Tested against who actually made it

Which VFL players are closest to AFL footy? Each player gets a percentage: his chance of playing AFL the following season. It blends how good he is in the VFL, what his numbers project to at AFL level, his age and how much he's played. Only about 1 in 12 VFL players who haven't played AFL get a game the next year, so most numbers are low: the typical VFL player sits at 4%. This page shows the standouts, every player at 30% or more.

How the score works

Four things go in. VFL level is the player's VFL WAR per game as a percentile against his position (the bar shows the percentile). AFL projection converts his VFL numbers to an AFL line, using how much each stat shrank for players who moved up, then runs that line through the AFL version of the model. The bar shows that as projected AFL WAR over 20 games. Age matters because younger players with the same output have more room to grow, and clubs back them. Games gives more trust to bigger samples.

The blend comes from a model trained on what happened next. For every VFL player in 2024 and 2025 who hadn't played AFL, we know whether he got AFL games the following year. The weights are set to predict that, and we tested it fairly: a model trained only on 2024 was checked against 2025, and the other way round, so it never saw the answers it was graded on. The number shown is that predicted chance. It's checked against reality in the chart at the top: across 2024 and 2025, players given 30% or more played AFL the next year 49% of the time, and players under 5% did 2% of the time.

For players who already played AFL that season, the chance is shown for comparison but the model was built on players who hadn't. Two limits. The score can't see list status, so a great VFL player who isn't on an AFL list still needs a club to draft him. And age carries real weight because clubs give games to young players. Older players can still be ready; the VFL level and AFL projection bars show that side on its own.

"Projects like" lists the three 2026 AFL players at the same position whose numbers are closest to his projected AFL line. Bracketed numbers are their 2026 DAM WAR.
