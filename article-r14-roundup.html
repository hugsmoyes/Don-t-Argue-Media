YOUR CLUB'S BEST:
ROUND 14

WAR v36  ·  2026 Season

Four clubs had the bye, but the seven games that did get played produced one of the heaviest ruck rounds of the year. Jordan Dawson's 32-disposal, three-goal demolition of the Bulldogs is the standout of the round, and it isn't close. Behind him, Jordon Sweet and Max Gawn both turned in performances big enough to carry their side on their own, while Liam Ryan's six-goal salvo up forward kept St Kilda's night alive against the Giants.

Adelaide  ·  W 118–63 vs Western Bulldogs
Jordan Dawson 0.436
32d  ·  3g  ·  8t  ·  7 cl  ·  12 si

Thirty-two disposals, three goals, eight tackles and six inside 50s in a 55-point demolition. Dawson was at the centre of everything for Adelaide, adding 12 score involvements and seven clearances on top of it. Sam Berry's 11 clearances kept the midfield humming behind him, but this was Dawson's afternoon from start to finish. The best individual round of the season so far, in a result that was never in doubt.

Port Adelaide  ·  L 86–89 vs Sydney
Jordon Sweet 0.311
35 hitouts  ·  9 cl  ·  13 si  ·  2g  ·  8 sl

Nine clearances, 35 hitouts and 13 score involvements from the ruck, with two goals on top. Sweet was enormous all day, the kind of performance that usually wins games on its own. Zak Butters added 27 disposals and six intercepts trying to drag Port over the line. It wasn't enough - Sydney snuck home by three - but Sweet's afternoon deserves to be remembered regardless of the result.

Melbourne  ·  W 91–46 vs Essendon
Max Gawn 0.293
29 hitouts  ·  10 cl  ·  2g  ·  5 ic  ·  7 si

Two goals, ten clearances, five intercepts and 29 hitouts from the ruck in a 45-point win. Gawn's influence stretched from the centre bounce to deep in defence all afternoon. Kysaiah Pickett kicked three goals and racked up 32 disposals of his own, but Gawn's was the more complete game - he was doing the heavy lifting that let everyone else play loose footy. Essendon had no answer for either of them.

St Kilda  ·  W 95–86 vs Greater Western Sydney
Liam Ryan 0.251
6g  ·  2b  ·  11d  ·  9 si  ·  2 cm

Six goals from just 11 disposals - Ryan was lethal in front of the sticks, adding two contested marks and nine score involvements off barely a touch count. Nasiah Wanganeen-Milera was the engine room story with 32 disposals and 775 metres gained, but it was Ryan's finishing that put the game away. Six majors is six majors, and St Kilda needed every one of them in a nine-point arm wrestle with the Giants.
