Wins Above VFL Replacement · 10 stats · Frozen 2025 VFL baseline · Within-position z-scored

VFL WAR measures how much value each VFL player adds over a replacement-level VFL player at his position. Every player is rated against a fixed 2025 VFL standard, then multiplied by games played, so it builds up across a season. A score of 0 is replacement level. This is the 2026 season. Players marked AFL games also played AFL that season; their VFL WAR only counts their VFL games.
