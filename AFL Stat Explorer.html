About the data

All numbers are per-game averages across the whole season, finals included. Because they're averages, a player who played finals isn't advantaged over one who didn't.

Shading shows where each number ranks. "vs position" compares a player only with others in his position who meet your filters, so a key defender's 4 marks is judged against key defenders, not midfielders. "vs all players" compares everyone with everyone. Green means above the middle, deeper green is closer to the top; pink means below the middle. For clangers, turnovers, frees against and one-on-one loss %, lower is better, so the colours flip. Turn it off for a plain table.

TOG-adjusted scales each counting stat up to a full game by dividing it by time on ground: a player with 20 disposals at 80% time on ground shows 20 ÷ 0.8 = 25. It's fairer to players who spend time on the bench or come on as the sub. Percentages, ratings and DAM WAR aren't adjusted, and players under 50% time on ground are hidden in this view because their numbers would blow out.

Every view can be shared. Copy link saves your season, filters, columns, sort and compared players into the address, so whoever opens it sees exactly what you see. Download CSV gives you the whole filtered table.

DAM WAR is a season total built on home and away games only, so it can't be compared directly with the per-game stats beside it. It's shown for 2025 and 2026; the 2024 season predates the current model.
