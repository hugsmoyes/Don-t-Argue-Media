About
Making AFL statistics accessible to everyone.

Don't Argue Media builds tools that let any fan explore the game through data — free, open, and designed to make sense without a statistics degree.

The flagship framework is Wins Above Replacement (WAR), collapsing a player's full contribution into one number. Radar profiles add the visual layer.
