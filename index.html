About

Don't Argue Media is an independent AFL analytics project built around a single question: what does the data actually say?

The centrepiece is a Wins Above Replacement (WAR) framework — a composite model that collapses a player's full statistical contribution into a single number, denominated in wins. WAR is position-adjusted, opponent-adjusted, and built from the ground up on AFL-specific data rather than borrowed from other sports.

Alongside WAR, the project produces player radar profiles across the AFL — visualisation tools designed to surface the shape of a player's game, not just their counting stats. The full analysis tool exposes every assumption and derivation step in the model pipeline.

Everything is independently produced and built in Python. Update this section with your own story.
