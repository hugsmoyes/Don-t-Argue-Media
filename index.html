About
Making AFL statistics accessible to everyone. Fans, coaches, analysts. No jargon required.

AFL has some of the richest statistical data in world sport. Most of it stays locked behind jargon or complex papers. Don't Argue Media exists to change that.

The goal is simple: build tools that let any fan explore the game through data. Whether you've never heard of WAR or you're writing your own models, everything here is free, open and designed to make sense without a statistics degree.

The first framework is Wins Above Replacement (WAR), a model that collapses a player's full contribution into a single number measured in wins. Alongside WAR, radar profiles give a visual shape to how any player competes across multiple dimensions.
