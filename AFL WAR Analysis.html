21 per-game stats are z-scored against a frozen 2025-season baseline population (±3.0 cap), multiplied by category weights to form a composite, then within-position z-scored against the same frozen 2025 baseline so each position group is measured against its own peers. Three stats are derived from raw inputs. Because the baseline is fixed, a player's WAR only moves when their own season-average numbers or games played change - not because the rest of the competition had a big week.

Wheelo Ratings (RatingPoints_Avg) is Champion Data's per-game composite rating, measuring ball involvement, efficiency and impact. The WAR model takes a different approach. Players are judged within their position group against a frozen 2025 replacement-level baseline, then multiplied by games played - so WAR accumulates like a counting stat, the way games and goals do. Green = WAR ranks the player higher. Red = Wheelo ranks the player higher.
